# Persona — Patient

> A person who uses the **Balsm app** to manage their own health — booking appointments, viewing records, ordering medications, and staying connected with their care team.

---

## Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **App** | Balsm (patient-facing, multi-platform) |
| **Account type** | Patient account (not a Balsm Pro user) |
| **Arabic label** | بلسم |
| **Platforms** | iOS, Android, Web, macOS, Windows, Linux |

---

## Who They Are

Patients are individuals managing their personal health. They may be:

- A person with a chronic condition tracking ongoing care
- Someone booking a one-off consultation or lab test
- A family member managing health for a relative (see [Caregiver](./caregiver))
- A walk-in customer at a pharmacy who has linked their Balsm account

Patients are **not** staff of a healthcare entity. They interact with the platform as consumers, not practitioners.

---

## Goals

- Book appointments with doctors, labs, or scan centers
- Access their medical records and test results
- Order medications and track prescription refills
- Receive reminders for upcoming appointments and medication schedules
- Communicate securely with their care team
- Share health data with trusted family members or caregivers

---

## Pain Points

- Fragmented records scattered across multiple providers and paper files
- Difficulty tracking medication schedules and refills
- No visibility into appointment status or wait times
- Having to re-explain their history at every new provider visit
- Inability to delegate health management to a caregiver without sharing credentials

---

## Key Features / Workflows

| Workflow | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Medical Records** | View, download, and share health history across providers |
| **Appointments** | Book, reschedule, and cancel appointments; receive reminders |
| **Prescriptions** | View active prescriptions; scan QR codes at pharmacies |
| **Medication Orders** | Order medications from network pharmacies with delivery or pickup |
| **Lab Results** | View lab test results as soon as they are released |
| **Notifications** | Appointment reminders, medication alerts, test result notifications |
| **Family / Dependents** | Add family members or dependents; delegate access to a caregiver |
| **Provider Search** | Browse and book from the Balsm Network directory |
| **Sharing** | Generate shareable health summary links (time-limited, consent-controlled) |

---

## Data & Privacy

- Patients control who can access their records via consent management
- All PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit
- Patients can revoke provider access at any time
- Dependent patient accounts are managed by a guardian until the dependent turns 18 — see [Caregiver](./caregiver)

---

## Related Personas

- [Caregiver](./caregiver) — manages health on behalf of a patient (child or dependent)
- [Doctor](./doctor) — views patient records during consultations
- [Pharmacist](./pharmacist) — dispenses medications to the patient
- [Receptionist](./receptionist) — books appointments on the patient's behalf
