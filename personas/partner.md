# Persona — Partner

> A representative of a pharmaceutical company, insurance provider, research institution, or other healthcare partner who uses **Balsm Connect** to access analytics, run campaigns, and engage with the Balsm provider network.

---

## Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **App** | Balsm Connect |
| **Account type** | Partner account (not a Balsm Pro user) |
| **Arabic label** | بلسم - شركاء |
| **Platforms** | Web only |

---

## Who They Are

Partners are organizations that collaborate with Balsm to reach healthcare providers, conduct research, or offer value-added services to entities on the platform. Partner types include:

| Partner Type | Primary Use Case |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Pharmaceutical Company** | Run physician-targeted educational campaigns; track medication adoption trends |
| **Insurance Provider** | Offer co-pay programs and coverage verification to entities on the network |
| **Research Institution** | Access anonymized, consent-gated population health data for clinical research |
| **Medical Device Manufacturer** | Promote products to entities and manage device-linked service programs |

Partners interact with the platform through **Balsm Connect**, a separate web portal. They do **not** have access to Balsm Pro or individual patient records. All data access is consent-gated and de-identified unless explicit consent is obtained.

---

## Goals

- Reach relevant healthcare professionals with targeted, compliant campaigns
- Access aggregate analytics on prescribing trends and treatment patterns (de-identified)
- Manage vouchers and co-pay programs for patients
- Track campaign performance and ROI in real time
- Submit research data access requests with full consent documentation

---

## Pain Points

- Difficulty targeting the right specialist segment without wasting campaign budget
- No feedback loop between campaign spend and actual prescription behavior
- Research data requests requiring months of paperwork and approval cycles
- Co-pay and voucher programs managed through external systems with no integration into the pharmacy workflow

---

## Key Features / Workflows

| Workflow | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Analytics Dashboard** | View aggregate, de-identified trends — prescribing patterns, diagnosis frequency, geographic distribution |
| **Campaign Management** | Create, schedule, and manage educational campaigns targeting providers by specialty, region, or entity type |
| **Voucher Programs** | Issue patient vouchers or co-pay subsidies that are redeemable directly at Balsm pharmacies |
| **Campaign Analytics** | Track impressions, engagement, redemption rates, and conversion metrics |
| **Research Access Requests** | Submit data access requests for de-identified population health datasets with consent documentation |
| **Provider Directory** | Browse the Balsm network provider directory to identify potential campaign targets |
| **Account & Billing** | Manage partner account details, subscription tier, and campaign spend |

---

## Data Access Rules

- Partners **never** access individually identifiable patient data without explicit, logged consent
- All analytics data is **de-identified and aggregated** at minimum
- Research data access is subject to a formal request and approval process
- All partner data access is logged in the immutable audit trail
- Campaign delivery to providers is opt-in — providers control whether they receive partner content

## Related Personas
